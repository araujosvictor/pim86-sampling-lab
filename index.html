Distribution of sample proportions

The standard error is the standard deviation of the sampling distribution. For a proportion under independent Bernoulli sampling, SE = √[p(1 − p) / n]. Larger n means a narrower sampling distribution.

Standard normal distribution

The central limit theorem explains why averages of sufficiently large independent, identically distributed samples with finite variance are approximately normally distributed. For proportions, the approximation improves when expected success and failure counts are sufficiently large.

A 95% confidence procedure aims to capture the fixed true parameter in approximately 95% of repeated samples. Once an interval is calculated, the parameter is not random in the frequentist interpretation.
